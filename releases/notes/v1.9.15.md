The byte ruler got stricter and found a real one straight away: after about ten minutes of fighting the
game was throwing away forty times the garbage it had always been measured at.
- **Why a long fight got hitchy.** Every spark on the glass is dragged a little each step, and the three
  drag numbers used were looked up out of one object keyed by the shape's name. As long as the compiler
  could see those numbers were plain doubles, fetching them cost nothing; around ten minutes of arena
  time it stopped being able to prove it, and every fetch started handing back a boxed number - twice a
  spark, with 900 sparks on the cap that is **29 KB a step instead of 716 bytes**, a young generation
  filled every third of a second and a collector to sit through each time. Three named lookups and two
  comparisons do the same job and cannot box. A run that has been going since the first boss is the one
  that was paying for it.
- **What made it visible.** `tools/alloc-probe.js` warms a load up before weighing it, and it used to
  stop as soon as the readings levelled off. A load does not level off: it falls in steps, each one flat
  for thousands of steps and each one cheaper than the last - so two processes weighing the same crowd
  reported 289, 369 or 410 bytes a step depending on which step of the fall they happened to stop on,
  which is why that file's checks went red on commits that had not touched a byte. The warm-up now ends
  when the cheapest reading has stopped falling *and* the readings agree, and the same crowd reads **241
  B a step in every process** - idle, or with 28 hogs on 32 cores.
- **And the numbers came down with it**: the empty arena 64 → **16 B** a step (most of the old floor was
  this one lookup), a full crowd about a fifth cheaper, the dearest gated load **713 B** against its 896
  budget, and the floor budget came down 128 → 48 B so that this class of bug is caught at the floor from
  now on. Putting the old lookup back reads 29 KB and fails the gate, which is how every budget here gets
  checked.
- **Nothing you see moved.** The same seed plays the same run, byte for byte - a drag has never fed back
  into a run. 2,619 checks pass.
