A new enemy: the **MENDER**.
- **A violet cross that patches the crowd.** It arrives at 8:20, rare, and stands off at 170-270 px.
  Every 2.6 seconds it heals the most wounded *other* body within 120 px by 2 hull - the lowest share
  of its own hull first, never itself - and a mint ring is the tell. Killing the one doing it is the
  answer; shooting the crowd through it is not.
- **A crowd that helps itself for the first time.** The roster had every way to hurt the ship and no
  way for the crowd to keep one of its own standing. The mender is a new decision, not a new damage
  system.
- The reference run is now **74,748 / level 57 / 3,997 kills / 5,068 shards** (was 73,354 / 3,898 /
  4,998); a four-run batch all survives the cap (median score 67,703). Shape, palette, codex, wave
  clock and heal tests all know it.
