A new enemy: the PULSAR.
- **A pink five-point star that marks your ground.** It keeps its distance, and every 3.4 seconds it
  draws a dashed circle where you are standing, waits 0.9 seconds, then drops a blast there (radius
  96, damage 5, shove 220). The circle is the promise: walk out of it and the blast costs nothing.
- **It stands still under its own mark** and can be caught in the blast if you lead the circle back
  over the star. It arrives from 9:20, cost 8, weight 1, so it is a late-run problem rather than
  weather.
- The mark is paid by its own pass after the crowd has walked, so the blast can never remove bodies
  while the crowd loop is reading them. The enemy keeps no new fields: the marked point reuses the
  direction pair and a `ready` stage, and the empty-arena alloc floor stays at 16 B.
- Five tests in `test/pulsar.test.mjs`; the reference run is `74,459 / level 57 / 3,938 kills /
  5,070 shards / hull 2` (the blast's own kills are unpaid; the run is alive at the cap).
