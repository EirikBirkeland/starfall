A real-browser audit of the screens the stub DOM cannot see found the one card that did not fit.
- **A late wreck or win no longer runs its bottom off the glass.** The ending card is the tallest thing in
  the game - a full build, every hull written down, the day, the rule, the awards - and as a bare flex
  column it put its restart hint on top of the XP bar at `1280x720` (the win's `PRESS SPACE TO KEEP
  FLYING` was drawn through it). Both endings are now one body (`.end-body`) and are fitted to the glass
  the HUD leaves them, the same way the hangar, codex, pause and settings panels already were. A late
  card scales to about 0.88 on a desktop and lands clear of the XP bar; the run itself is byte for byte
  what it was.
- **A phone ending card clears the HUD.** On a portrait phone the title started under the top-left score
  and clock, the long challenge and stats lines ran under the left HUD rail, and the hint crowded the
  bottom. The ending screens now reserve the top strip and the left rail (and a narrower card body under
  640 px), so a `390x844` card starts below the HUD and right of the rail; the win's invitation re-fits
  the card on the frame it arrives.
- **The page ships its own tab icon** - the same ship dart the game draws, inline as an SVG - so the
  browser stops asking for a `favicon.ico` that was never there and the console is clean on a first visit.
